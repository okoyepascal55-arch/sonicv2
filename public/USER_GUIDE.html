Sonic Group Website

User Guide

Everything you need to know about using and managing the Sonic Group website — no technical knowledge required.

👋What is this website?

The Sonic Group website (sonic-group.de) is the official online home of Sonic Group — a professional agency that helps brands enter and grow in the German, Austrian, and Swiss (DACH) markets.

The website has two main purposes:

  • Public website — tells brands and job seekers who Sonic Group is, what services are offered, and how to get in touch.
  • Content Dashboard — a behind-the-scenes tool where the team can update photos and text on the website without needing a developer.
💡 Good to know: The website is written in German (Deutsch) and targets a German-speaking audience in the DACH region.

🖥️What is the Content Dashboard?

The Content Dashboard is a private admin area accessible at sonic-group.de/dashboard. It is only for the Sonic Group team and is not visible to normal website visitors.

It allows authorised team members to:

  • ✅ Change photos on any page of the website
  • ✅ Edit text, headings, and copy on any page
  • ✅ Upload new images from your computer
  • ✅ Reorganise the order of images
  • ✅ Reset any section back to its default content

Changes made in the Dashboard appear on the live website immediately — no waiting, no developer needed.

🖼️Updating Images

Once logged in, you will see the dashboard with a sidebar on the left listing all the pages (Home, Lösungen, Leistungen, Über uns, etc.) and a tab bar at the top switching between Medien (images) and Text.

Finding the right section

1
Click the page in the sidebar

For example, click "Home" to manage Home page images.

2
Make sure "Medien" is selected

Click the "Medien" tab in the top bar if it's not already active (it will have a lime-green background when selected).

3
Click on a section to expand it

You will see a list of image sections (e.g. "Hero — Stats", "Trust Strip Logos"). Click on one to open it and see the images inside.

Adding an image by URL

1
Open the section

Click the section name to expand it.

2
Click the "URL" button

A small form will appear.

3
Paste the image link

Copy an image URL from the web and paste it in.

4
Add a caption (optional)

Give the image a name or description. This is used for accessibility.

5
Click Add / Hinzufügen

The image appears in the section immediately.

Deleting an image

1
Hover over the image card

A set of small buttons will appear in the top-left corner of the image.

2
Click the red bin icon 🗑️

A confirmation popup will ask "Are you sure?"

3
Confirm deletion

Click Yes / Ja to permanently remove the image from that section.

Reordering images

You can change the order of images by dragging and dropping them:

1
Hover over an image

You will see a drag handle icon (⠿) in the top-left corner.

2
Click and hold the handle

The image will slightly shrink to show it's being dragged.

3
Drag it to the new position

Release it over another image to swap positions. The order is saved automatically.

Resetting a section

If you want to undo all changes to a section and go back to the original images:

  1. Open the section by clicking on it
  2. Click the "Zurücksetzen" (Reset) button
  3. The section reverts to its default images
⚠️ Warning: Resetting a section is permanent. All custom images you added will be removed and you cannot undo this action.

📤Uploading Photos from Your Computer

You can upload photos directly from your computer to appear on the website. The system automatically compresses large photos so the website stays fast.

Single photo upload

1
Open the image section

Click on the section where you want to add the photo.

2
Click the "Upload" button

This opens a file picker on your computer.

3
Select your photo

Choose the file from your computer. Maximum file size is 10 MB.

4
Add a caption (optional)

Give the photo a name. The filename is automatically used if you leave this blank.

5
Click Add / Hinzufügen

The photo uploads to the website and appears in the section.

Bulk upload (multiple photos at once)

1
Open the section and click "Bulk"

The Bulk Upload panel opens.

2
Select multiple photos

Either click to browse or drag-and-drop multiple images into the upload area.

3
Set a caption prefix (optional)

This adds a shared label to all photos, e.g. "Team Event" → "Team Event 1", "Team Event 2", etc.

4
Click Upload

A progress bar shows how many photos have been uploaded. Any failures are listed so you can retry.

Replacing an existing photo

You can swap out an existing image with a new one while keeping its position in the section:

  1. Hover over the image you want to replace
  2. Click the image edit icon (📝 with a picture) — it looks like a photo with a pencil
  3. Select your new photo from your computer
  4. The old photo is replaced automatically
💡 Good to know about photo sizes: The system automatically resizes large photos to a maximum width of 1920 pixels and converts them to the efficient WebP format. You don't need to resize photos before uploading — just upload the original and the system handles the rest. The maximum upload size is 10 MB per photo.

✏️Updating Text & Copy

You can edit headings, descriptions, button labels, statistics, and other text across the website directly in the dashboard.

1
Select the page from the sidebar

Click on the page whose text you want to edit (e.g. Home).

2
Click the "Text" tab

In the top bar, click "Text" (with the document icon) to switch to the text editor.

3
Open the section you want to edit

Click on the section name (e.g. "Hero — HeroRevamp Section") to expand it.

4
Find the text entry you want to change

Each entry has a label (like "H1 Line 1" or "Subtitle") to help you identify what it controls.

5
Type your new text

Click into the text box and edit the content. Longer texts have a bigger text area.

6
Click Save / Speichern

The new text is saved and appears on the live website immediately.

Resetting text to defaults

If you accidentally change something and want the original text back, click the reset button next to the entry or use the "Zurücksetzen" button for the whole section.

ℹ️ Note: Each text entry has a small label showing what type it is — for example Heading, CTA, Stat. This helps you understand what part of the page you are editing.

💡Tips & Best Practices

Image quality tips

  • Use high-quality photos (at least 1000px wide) — the system handles compression automatically
  • For logos and icons, use SVG files when possible — they stay crisp at any size
  • For hero banners and backgrounds, use wide landscape images (wider than they are tall)
  • For team photos and portraits, portrait or square images work best
  • The dashboard shows a recommended size hint (e.g. "Ideal: 1920×1080") next to each section — use this as a guide

Text editing tips

  • Keep headings short and impactful — the design is built around concise text
  • Statistics and numbers (e.g. "1.35M+") are often separate fields so they can be styled differently
  • Button labels (CTA) should be action words — e.g. "Jetzt starten", "Mehr erfahren"

General tips

  • Changes take effect immediately on the live website — always check the live site after making changes
  • The Dashboard works best on a desktop computer or laptop — it can be used on a tablet but is not designed for phones
  • You can have the live website open in another tab while making changes in the dashboard — refresh the live tab to see updates
  • If you see a broken image icon (⚠️) in the dashboard, it means the image URL is no longer valid. Use the Replace button to swap it with a new one

What to do if something goes wrong

  • If you accidentally delete an image, use the Reset section button to restore all defaults
  • If text looks wrong on the live site, go back to the Text panel and restore the original wording
  • If the upload fails, check that your photo is under 10 MB and try again
  • If you are locked out of the dashboard, contact your team administrator for the correct credentials
